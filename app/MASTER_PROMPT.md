# XDS Recruitment Management System

## Overview

This application is a complete recruitment management platform for XDS.

The application should be modern, minimal and premium.

Inspired by Apple, Linear and Notion.

Dark theme.

Elegant animations.

No unnecessary clutter.

---

## Roles

There are four roles.

1. Admin
2. Organizer (OC)
3. Judge
4. Participant

---

## Recruitment Flow

Participant arrives.

↓

Organizer selects participant.

↓

Organizer assigns:

- Motion
- Stance
- Preparation Desk (optional)

↓

Generate QR.

↓

Participant scans QR.

↓

10-minute preparation timer begins.

↓

Organizer dashboard monitors timer live.

↓

If participant leaves the page:

Record timestamp.

Notify organizer.

↓

At timer end:

Alarm plays.

Judge desk is revealed.

↓

Participant proceeds to assigned judge desk.

↓

Judge logs into Judge Dashboard.

↓

Judge opens participant.

↓

Judge evaluates Speech.

↓

Judge evaluates Personal Interview.

↓

Judge evaluates Skills Interview.

↓

Judge submits.

↓

Organizer instantly sees final scores.

↓

Recruitment history stores everything.

Judge cannot edit other desks.

Judge submits scores digitally.

Everything autosaves.
